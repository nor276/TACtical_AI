**Disagreement:** Designers 2, 3, 5, 10 split WeaponSpec real-values from honest-geometry across phases (their P1 weapons, P2 chassis-physics). Designers 1, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9 bundle 1+2+7 in P1.

### Q2 — Where does item 37 (per-EmitterKind multipliers) belong?

**Disagreement:** Designer 1 placed in P4 (alongside ThrustField mods). Designers 2, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 placed in P2 or P9 (with threading tunables). Designer 3 placed in P9. Designer 5 placed in P9 (tools+tests).
